# Baliyatra 2025 Website

A beautiful, modern website celebrating Odisha's maritime heritage and the famous Baliyatra festival. Inspired by the Argus News tourism website design, this site showcases the rich cultural traditions and historical significance of one of India's largest open-air fairs.

## 🌟 Features

### Design & User Experience
- **Modern, Responsive Design** - Optimized for all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Beautiful Animations** - Smooth transitions, floating elements, and interactive effects
- **Baliyatra Theme** - Custom color scheme inspired by Odisha's maritime heritage
- **Professional Typography** - Clean, readable fonts with proper hierarchy

### Interactive Elements
- **Smooth Scrolling Navigation** - Seamless navigation between sections
- **Mobile-Friendly Menu** - Hamburger menu for mobile devices
- **Gallery Lightbox** - Click to view images in full-screen mode
- **Contact Form** - Functional contact form with validation
- **Back-to-Top Button** - Easy navigation to top of page

## 🎉 Festival Information

Baliyatra (literally "voyage to Bali") is one of India's largest open-air fairs and the world's second-largest trade fair. It commemorates the ancient maritime trade between Odisha and Southeast Asian countries, particularly Bali, Java, and Sumatra.

### Key Details:
- **Location**: Cuttack, Odisha, India
- **Duration**: November 15-30, 2025
- **Visitors**: Over 2 million annually
- **Stalls**: 5000+ exhibitions and stalls
- **History**: 1000+ years of maritime tradition
